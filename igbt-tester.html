IGBT Functionality Tester

Our IGBT Functionality Tester is engineered to streamline power unit diagnostics, enabling technicians to test IGBTs directly within the unit and identify faults without unnecessary disassembly. Issues are pinpointed swiftly and accurately — saving valuable time on every service call.

Not only does our tester confirm faulty components, it also verifies that replacement IGBTs are fully operational before reinstallation, enhancing reliability and preventing costly repeat failures. Trusted by operations at Delta Air Lines and FedEx.
